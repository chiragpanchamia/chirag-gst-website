Frequently Asked GST Questions

Can you help analyse a GST notice?

Yes. A notice can be structured into factual discrepancies, applicable provisions, supporting documents, calculations and response points.

Can you review ITC mismatch issues?

Yes. Reconciliation can compare books, purchase registers, GSTR-2B and returns to identify excess, short or timing differences.

Can businesses get monthly GST compliance support?

Yes. A workflow can cover return-data review, reconciliation, issue identification and filing-readiness checks.

Do you provide GST learning resources?

Yes. The website and YouTube channel focus on practical, section-wise GST education in Hindi.
